## Verified vs not — be precise

**Verified**: against its own built-in 75% bar generator, by reading pixels back off the GL
canvas. Every bar's waveform level matches its BT.709 luma to within the readback's row
quantisation (white 74.8 vs 75.00, blue 5.4 vs 5.42); the vectorscope trace lands on its
cyan/green/blue targets to within a pixel; the histogram spikes at 0 and 75 IRE. That proves the
maths, uniforms, graticule derivation and capture→texture→scope path agree end to end.

**Not verified**: no ATEM has ever been connected, no real multiview captured, and the Electron
app has never been *launched* (see [npm postinstall blocked](https://github.com/stoatworks-labs/fleet-notes/blob/main/notes/reference_npm_postinstall_blocked.md) — no Electron binary
here, so "all three bundles build" is the only claim). `native/decklink` has **never been
compiled**: the DeckLink SDK is licence-gated and is not on this machine.

## CI has a shader-compile check, and it earned its place

`scripts/check-shaders.sh` loads the built page in headless Chrome + SwiftShader and greps for
compile/link failures. Added after a **`half` reserved-word bug reached the live site**: GLSL is
just a string until a driver sees it, so it passed typecheck, lint, tests and the bundle. Also
guards the invariant that no ATEM code enters the hosted bundle. Do not try to assert *what was
drawn* under SwiftShader — its float blending does not accumulate, so the histogram is wrong
there while being right on a real GPU.
